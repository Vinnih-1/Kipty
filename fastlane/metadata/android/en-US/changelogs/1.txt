This release focuses on FOSS compliance ahead of the F-Droid submission, alongside speech recognition improvements and several UI fixes.

- Improved speech recognition by using detailed pronunciation results and better word matching
- Fixed transcription splitting to use word limits and time gaps
- Fixed double-click issue when starting a recording by adding a minimum recording duration
- Replaced Google Fonts with locally bundled font resources
- Replaced the "Speak Easy" sample audio with the public domain "Historical Newspaper Articles, Volume 1" collection from LibriVox
- Removed the unused whisper.cpp module
- Fixed comprehensive UI issues on small screen widths
- Fixed file extension incorrectly included in audio names in PopulateWorker
